## 3. Page-by-page content

### Home
Story of the founding: first event was a 2015 high-school conference on **Shine, Grow, Share**; a speaker asked "what are next year's themes?" and the ministry was born. Since then: youth camps, annual conferences, a worship team, college ministry. CTAs: newsletter signup, college, blog, give, merch. Socials banner image.

### About
Culture offers teens endless competing beliefs; adolescence is when lifelong decisions are made. Dare prepares young believers for a lifetime of ministry, calls older believers to model standing on Scripture, and stresses unity around the work of Christ, partnering with local churches on conferences and reaching the suffering and doubting. Image: IMG_7561.

### What We Believe
Scripture inspired, accurate, the ultimate foundation for worldview. God created all things; humans made in His image but rebelled. Sin brings eternal separation. Jesus is God's only begotten Son, incarnate, sinless Savior; faith in Christ alone reconciles. Christ is building His kingdom, will return for His bride, and has won the victory.

### Mission and Vision
Prepare young Saints for a lifetime of ministry; unify like-minded believers; reach the next generation of Church leaders by upholding truth in a secular culture through conferences and online resources. CTA: "Questions? Ask Us Here".

## 4. What's working
- A clear, memorable mission and a strong name ("Dare") with a built-in values acronym.
- A real track record: 8+ conferences, real speakers and churches, a Uganda partnership, a 15-person podcast team.
- Giving and forms already run through Breeze, so a static front end can hand off cleanly.

## 5. Problems to fix
1. **Stale and hidden content.** Newest blog post is 2021, newsletter stops at 2021, conferences stop at 2021, College Activities is empty. The newest work (the 2024 podcast, Uganda) is only reachable from the footer or Linktree.
2. **Navigation doesn't match the ministry.** Conferences, Worship, Podcast and Uganda aren't in the menu; "Our Staff" lives at /partners/; an empty "Ministries" hub page.
3. **No visual identity.** Default WordPress theme, small logo, few photos, no consistent color or type.
4. **Weak calls to action.** Generic share buttons on every page; no single "next step" per audience (student, parent, church, donor).
5. **Inconsistent facts.** Two different addresses; dated events with no year (Uganda "December"); apologetics class from 2021 still reads as upcoming.
6. **Accessibility and performance.** Many images without alt text; heavy WordPress plugin load.
