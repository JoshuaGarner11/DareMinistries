A note first

To the Dare Ministries team,

Thank you for trusting me with the Dare Ministries website. It has been a joy to build something that tells the story of what God is doing through this ministry, and I want it to serve you well for years.

Before we go further, I want to make this easy and comfortable for both of us. I'm happy to do this as a gift, and I'm also glad to do it as paid work if that fits the ministry's budget better. Below are a few simple options. There is no wrong answer, and none of them changes how much I care about getting this right.

Joshua

Ten years of ministry, finally in one place.

I read every page of the current site. The ministry has grown far past what the site shows.

The site today

  • The newest blog post and newsletter are from 2021
  • The podcast and Uganda pages are only reachable from the footer or Linktree
  • Conferences and Worship aren't in the menu
  • The College Activities page has no activities on it
  • Two different mailing addresses on two pages
  • Many photos have no descriptions for screen readers

The new site

  • Every ministry one tap away: Conferences, College, Worship, the podcast and Uganda
  • A scrolling timeline of every conference since 2015, with speakers and videos
  • A clear next step for students, college students, churches and donors
  • A sponsor-a-student calculator for the Uganda conference
  • Giving buttons linked straight to each Breeze fund
  • Built for phones first, and fast on any connection

Three ways we can do this.

Pick whichever fits the ministry best. A site like this typically runs $4,000 to $5,000 from a design agency, so every option is a real gift to the ministry.

A gift

$0

  • The full new site, launched and connected to your domain
  • One round of fixes after launch, then as my schedule allows
  • Timeline fit around my other commitments
  • No payment

A love offering

Whatever the ministry feels led to give

  • The full new site, launched and connected to your domain
  • One round of fixes after launch, then as my schedule allows
  • Timeline fit around my other commitments
  • Any time, any amount

Ministry rate

$2,000 flat

  • The full new site, plus a training session
  • Unlimited small changes for 30 days after launch
  • Priority, with a set launch date
  • Half to start, half at launch

If you're not sure, the gift is completely fine. I would rather the ministry have a great website than have money be a reason to wait.

Optional: a monthly care plan.

If you'd like someone to keep the site fresh after launch, a care plan works with any option above. Start, pause or cancel any time.

Basic care

$50 / month

  • Up to 1 hour of updates a month: events, text, photos
  • A monthly check that everything works

Full care

$100 / month

  • Up to 3 hours of updates a month
  • New pages as needed
  • Quick turnaround on time-sensitive changes

Without a plan, one-off updates are $50 an hour, or I can show your team how to make simple changes yourselves.

Ongoing costs, whichever you choose.

The only ongoing cost is your domain name, about $15 a year, which the ministry likely already pays.

Hosting is free through GitHub Pages, and giving keeps running through Breeze exactly as it does today, so there are no new subscriptions.

Next steps

Just let me know which option feels right, and whether you'd like the care plan. A quick text or reply is plenty. If you'd rather talk it through over coffee, I'd love that too.

Whatever you choose, it's an honor to be part of what God is doing through Dare Ministries.

Grateful to serve with you,

Joshua
