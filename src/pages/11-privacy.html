Your trust matters.

Protecting your private information is our priority. This policy applies to www.dareministries.com and to Dare Ministries.

What we collect

This website does not use accounts, analytics cookies or advertising trackers. We only receive personal information you choose to send us, such as your name, email address, phone number or mailing address when you email us, fill out a form, register for an event or give.

Forms and giving

Registration forms, newsletter sign-ups and online giving are handled by our church management provider, Breeze ChMS (dareministries.breezechms.com). Information you submit there is governed by their privacy practices as well as ours. Embedded videos are served by YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you press play.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, to provide the ministry services you ask for, to send updates you've signed up for and to issue giving receipts. We do not sell or rent your information.
