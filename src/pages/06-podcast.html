About the show

Honest questions deserve honest answers.

Why is there so much pain? Where is God when life falls apart? Can I trust Him with my doubts?

Yet I Will Rejoice takes on the hard questions of life in a broken world, and keeps pointing to Jesus, who brings healing and redemption. The name comes from the prophet Habakkuk, who chose joy in God even when everything around him was failing.
