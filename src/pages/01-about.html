About Dare

Prepared for a lifetime of ministry.

A Colorado Springs ministry equipping young believers to stand on Scripture, and uniting churches to do it together.

Why we exist

A thousand voices. One truth.

Our culture offers young people endless options, including endless competing beliefs. Adolescence is when many of life's biggest decisions get made, and teenagers are hearing from schools, friends, media and sometimes even churches that don't stand on God's Word.

So we ask: is the church doing enough to reach the next generation with the Gospel?

Dare Ministries exists to prepare young Saints for a lifetime of ministry, and to call older believers to model what it looks like to stand on Scripture. We work alongside local churches, pool our resources for conferences and outreach, and reach out to those who are hurting or doubting, pointing them to Christ.

Mission and vision

Mission

Prepare young Saints for a lifetime of ministry.

We equip students to know what they believe, why they believe it, and how to live it out at school, at work and at play.

Vision

Unify the body of Christ around His work.

We bring like-minded believers and churches together to reach the next generation of Church leaders while upholding truth in an increasingly secular culture.

The journey

From one conference to a movement.

A few milestones from the first ten years.

  1. 2015The first Dare Conference: 100+ students, three dares (Shine, Grow, Share), and a question that started a ministry.
  2. 2017Dare College Ministries begins with a weekly Bible study for college-aged believers.
  3. 2018The first Recharge Conference, a mid-year boost for students, on living a "Life of Power."
  4. 2019The Upper Room Coffee House launches, and Dare partners with believers in Uganda for a youth conference.
  5. 2020Dare Ministries Worship and its internship launch; Truth in Two brings Bible truth in two minutes or less.
  6. 2021A 10-week apologetics class, and the Dare Conference theme "Dare to Be Dedicated."
  7. 2024Yet I Will Rejoice, a podcast for hard questions, launches with a 15-person volunteer team.

What we believe

Scripture is our foundation.

The convictions that every Dare conference, class and conversation is built on.

01The Word of God

The Bible is inspired by the Holy Spirit. The Old and New Testaments are accurate and true, and Scripture is the ultimate foundation for our worldview.

2 Timothy 3:16–17 · 2 Peter 1:20–21

02Creation and humanity

God created all things. Human beings were made in His image, but we have rebelled against Him.

Genesis 1:1, 1:27 · Romans 3:23

03Sin and its cost

Sin separates us from a holy God, and its penalty is eternal separation from Him.

Romans 6:23 · Isaiah 59:2

04Jesus Christ

Jesus is God's only begotten Son: God in the flesh, sinless, and the Savior of the world. Faith in Christ alone reconciles us to God.

John 1:14 · John 3:16 · Hebrews 4:15 · Ephesians 2:8–9

05The future

Christ is building His kingdom. One day He will return for His bride, the Church. The victory is already His.

Matthew 16:18 · John 14:3 · 1 Corinthians 15:57
