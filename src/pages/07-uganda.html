Uganda Youth Conference

One Church. Two continents.

Alongside national believers, we host a three-day youth conference in Uganda to help raise up a generation of church leaders.

Why Uganda

Hungry for the Gospel. Ready to partner.

Ugandan believers are eager for the Gospel and for partnership with the wider, global Church. We go to serve with them, not for them.

Together with national leaders we host a youth conference, and we encourage pastors at the national pastors' conference hosted by International Bible Conference (IBC).

  • Prepare students for ministry
  • Raise up church leaders
  • Build partnerships across continents
