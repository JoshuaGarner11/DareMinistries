It started with a question.

In the summer of 2015, Brandon Anderson gathered high schoolers from across Colorado Springs for a one-day conference built on three dares: Shine. Grow. Share.

Afterwards one of the speakers asked a simple question: "So what are next year's themes?" That question became a ministry. Since then Dare has hosted youth conferences and camps, launched a worship collective and a college Bible study, taught apologetics, partnered with believers in Uganda and started a podcast.

The goal has never changed: equip the next generation to stand on Scripture, and do it together, as one church.
